Hello from SYU CAMPUS.

**From October 4 through the early hours of October 5**, we improved several frequently used campus features. Here are the changes in one place.

## Graduation requirements now reflect the latest university materials

[Graduation Check](/academic/graduation) now includes **graduation requirements and course information from the university's 2026 academic handbook**. The handbook contains department curricula and the conditions needed for graduation.

- Check credits and required courses for your department and admission type.
- Where applicable, select your detailed major and the year in which you changed departments.
- When you change your department or admission conditions, the page first explains whether your entries will be reset.
- If progress cannot be saved automatically in your browser, a notice appears. You can keep your information by **exporting a file**.

Results are reference information to help you prepare for graduation. **Always verify the requirements that apply to you through the university sources shown on the page and your department office.**

## Festival night shuttles and holidays are easier to check

The [home page](/) and [Bus Info](/campus/bus-info) show **special festival night services for October 6**.

- **To Hwarangdae:** 5 buses in total between 22:00 and 22:30
- **To Byeollae:** 1 bus departing at 22:30

Individual departure times for Hwarangdae are not separately specified. Check university notices and on-site guidance before boarding.

The [Academic Calendar](/academic/schedule) now displays **public holidays and substitute holidays**. Shuttle guidance also accounts for holidays, reducing confusion caused by showing a normal timetable simply because a holiday falls on a weekday.

## Mobile input and timetable sharing improvements

- Fixed an issue that could close the keyboard or move the input position while searching for courses on a phone.
- The [Timetable Builder](/academic/timetable) shows when course information was updated.
- If a shared timetable is for a different semester from the current course data, the page explains this instead of loading it immediately. It also tells you when courses are missing from the current data.

## Notices when information is outdated

When some information, such as university notices or cafeteria menus, cannot be retrieved, the page **shows previously checked data and explains that the update is delayed**. Where available, it also shows the last successful update time.

Check the linked university source for important details such as application deadlines or menu changes.

## More stable notification settings

We reduced problems caused by repeatedly registering the same device or overlapping requests in a short time when enabling notifications. If you experienced notification setup problems, please check again in [Notifications and Privacy](/more/privacy).

Instructions for the dorm roommate board and privacy and terms information are provided in a [separate notice](/service/notices/017-roommate-board).

If you find a problem or incorrect information, please use **Contact the site** at the bottom of the page. We will keep improving the service based on your feedback.

Thank you.

## English summary

Here are the main improvements added on October 4 and early October 5, 2026.

- **Graduation checker:** Requirements and course data now follow the university's 2026 undergraduate handbook. Select your department, admission type, and applicable major or transfer year. The page asks before clearing your entries when you change those selections. If automatic saving is unavailable, export a file to keep your progress. Results are for reference; confirm the conditions that apply to you with the official sources and your department office.
- **Festival shuttle:** The home and bus-information pages show the October 6 special night services: five buses to Hwarangdae between 22:00 and 22:30, and one bus to Byeollae at 22:30. Individual Hwarangdae departure times are not specified. Check university notices and on-site guidance before boarding.
- **Public holidays:** The academic calendar includes public and substitute holidays. Shuttle guidance also accounts for holidays.
- **Mobile and timetable:** We fixed an issue that could interrupt mobile course-search input. The timetable shows course-data update information and warns when a shared timetable belongs to another semester or contains courses missing from the current data.
- **Delayed updates:** When new notices or cafeteria information cannot be collected, the page identifies the delay and shows previously verified data. Check the official source for important deadlines or changes.
- **Notifications:** We improved duplicate device registration and retry handling when multiple requests overlap.

Roommate-board instructions and privacy/terms information are available in the [separate notice](/service/notices/017-roommate-board). Please use **Contact** at the bottom of the page to report problems or incorrect information.
