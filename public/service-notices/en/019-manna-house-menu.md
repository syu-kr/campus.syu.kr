Hello from SYU CAMPUS.

You can now check menus for **Manna House, the dormitory cafeteria**, on SYU CAMPUS. Its menus are listed separately from **SU-Lounge**.

## Where can I find them?

- On the [home page](/), **Manna House below the cafeteria section** gives you a quick look at today's breakfast, lunch, and dinner.
- Use the **SU-Lounge / Manna House** buttons at the top of the [cafeteria page](/campus/cafeteria) to choose a restaurant.
- [Manna House menus](/campus/cafeteria?restaurant=manna) display **today's meals first and the current week's menu below**. Even if next week's menu is received, it stays hidden until that week begins.

A notice appears when today's menu is unavailable or its update cannot be verified. A menu from an earlier date is not shown as today's menu.

## How to read the menu information

Manna House menus are **extracted from menu images using AI**. Meals that are difficult to read or whose availability is unclear are marked **"Menu unavailable."** We have improved recognition to preserve the original dish names and wording, but errors may remain depending on the text and condition of the image.

The available materials do not specify exact serving times, so **breakfast, lunch, and dinner hours are all shown as "Unknown."**

You can also refer to **ingredient information** printed in the source alongside the menu. **A separate allergen list is not provided.** An ingredient list does not necessarily identify every allergen. If you have allergies, **check directly with the cafeteria before eating.**

Menus may change depending on conditions at the cafeteria. If you find incorrect information or have difficulty using the service, please use **Contact the site** at the bottom of the page.

Thank you.
