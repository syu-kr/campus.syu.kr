---

## New feature: Campus Tips resources

You can now find "Campus Tips" in the More menu.

We have collected information that students frequently need in one place.

- Official university websites and useful links
- Scholarships and financial information
- Certification examination schedules
- Competitions and extracurricular activities
- Job preparation resources
- Cultural activities and information about the surrounding area
- Communities for reference

To help you find what you need among the resources, we have also added **search and categories**.

---

## Usability improvements

We have refined several details to make the service easier to use, even if the changes are not immediately noticeable.

- **View the last update time** on information pages
- **Refresh reading-room information** at the library
- **Cleaner search results**

---

## Stability and performance improvements

We have made changes to improve stability throughout the service.

- **Faster use** by reducing unnecessary loading and delays
- Improvements to keep the service **comfortable to use when viewing a lot of information**
- Fixed a bug that **retrieved incorrect external information**
