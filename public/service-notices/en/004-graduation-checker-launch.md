Hello! We have added a new service to SYU CAMPUS.

Following a user suggestion, we have launched **Graduation Requirements Quick Check**.

---

## What is Graduation Requirements Quick Check?

Select your admission type, admission year, and department to see the requirements you need to graduate in one place.

### Main features

- **Support for admission types**: Graduation requirements for new students, transfer students, students changing departments, and other admission types
- **Support for the year of transfer**: For students changing departments, tailored guidance based on the timing of the transfer (the second semester of the first year, or the second, third, or fourth year)
- **Department-specific information**: Graduation requirements, required courses, and credit requirements for each department
- **Live calculations**: Enter completed courses to calculate remaining requirements automatically
- **Progress tracking**: A progress bar shows how far you have progressed toward graduation

---

## Important information

### This service is for reference

This service is **for reference**. The information may differ from your actual graduation requirements because of:

- Different academic regulations based on your admission year and major selection
- Special requirements for minors, double majors, teacher training, and other programs
- Department- and year-specific registration restrictions and prerequisites
- Changes to academic regulations and exceptional arrangements

### Verify the official information

**Always confirm the exact requirements through:**

1. **Your department office directly** (the most accurate source!)
2. The official graduation requirements in the SU-WINGs portal
3. An individual consultation with the academic affairs staff
