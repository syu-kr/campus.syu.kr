We plan to amend the inquiry and suggestion purposes in Article 1 and retention in Article 2 of the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

- Notice date: **November 4, 2026**
- Effective date: **December 5, 2026 at 00:00 Korean time**
- Scope: originals and included AI classification results for site inquiries and campus-tip suggestions
- Reason: to specify why past questions and suggestions are referenced, set a retention endpoint, and distinguish dedicated contact and browser-information cleanup from retaining original content.

## Existing purpose and period until the amendment takes effect

The existing purpose is **reviewing user submissions and inquiries and making service improvements**. The existing retention wording is:

> Contact requests and campus-tip suggestions are retained until the review or service-improvement purpose is fulfilled.

This purpose and period remain applicable until December 5, 2026 at 00:00 Korean time. Existing submissions require a separate check of their original purpose and lawful retention basis; this notice alone does not extend their retention. Any required separate consent or other procedure is not replaced by continued use or silence.

## Purpose and period applying from December 5

While a submission is being handled, it is kept for review and service improvement. After completion or rejection, its original content and included AI classification results are retained for **one year from completion or rejection** to **refer to previous questions and suggestions**.

After one year, the operator records only useful content after removing personal information and manually deletes the original and included AI results. Personal information in titles, content, links, notes, and AI results is reviewed, so originals with only dedicated fields removed are not copied unchanged into history records. No automatic purge is introduced.

Where separate retention is legally necessary, its basis, categories, and endpoint are checked, kept separate, and explained.

## Dedicated contact and browser-information fields are cleared separately

When completion/rejection review ends, the operator manually clears dedicated contact and browser-information (User-Agent) fields. This minimization is separate from retaining originals for one year; cleanup of dedicated fields that are no longer needed is not postponed until the amendment takes effect.

The original may still contain contact details, student numbers, or detailed URLs. Removing dedicated fields alone does not make the original anonymous or guarantee that all personal information has been removed.

## Completion dates and existing submissions

A completion date is recorded when a submission first enters completed or rejected status. Saving the same status or switching between completed and rejected does not extend that date. Reopening the submission for review clears it, and the next completion/rejection records a new date.

For existing submissions without a reliable completion date, the operator checks past status-transition audit evidence or handling records. Execution, creation, or last-update dates are not invented as completion dates. Submissions whose dates or retention basis cannot be established remain subject to separate review.

## Deletion requests and other amendment dates

You may request deletion or suspension before or after the new standard takes effect. Submit the request through the Contact button in the site footer or the [privacy contact email](mailto:singhic@outlook.kr). After identity verification, the operator processes the request or explains any restriction. Rights requests are handled separately from the individual-reply policy for general improvement suggestions.

The [October 4 amendment notice](/service/notices/017-roommate-board) and [November 3 clarification and further Terms amendment notice](/service/notices/021-policy-clarifications) remain preserved. The already announced November 4 and December 4 Terms effective dates are unchanged.
