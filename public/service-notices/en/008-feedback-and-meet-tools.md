Hello from SYU CAMPUS.

We have added several features to help gather useful campus information more quickly and arrange schedules together.

This update focuses on ways for users to participate directly.

---

## Contact the site

You can now report errors or suggest improvements directly within the site.

Previously, we accepted inquiries only by email. You can now submit them more easily through **Contact the site** at the bottom of the page.

You can contact us about:

- Page errors or bugs
- Requests to correct inaccurate information
- Suggestions for new features
- Difficulties you encountered while using the service
- Other service-related feedback

The operator reviews submissions and uses them to inform improvements.
At present, however, we cannot guarantee an individual reply.

---

## Suggest a campus tip

If you know information that would be useful on the Campus Tips page, you can submit it yourself.

Use **Suggest a campus tip** to send useful information you have learned during campus life, links you often visit, or tips you would like to share with newer students.

Examples include:

- Useful links for campus life
- Scholarship, finance, certification, and competition information
- Job preparation resources
- Information about life near campus
- Websites or resources students frequently use

Submissions are not published immediately. The operator reviews them and adds them to Campus Tips when appropriate.

---

## Schedule polls

We have added **Schedule Poll** to the More menu.

It gathers the times when people are available and helps you find the times that work for the most participants.

To use it:

1. Create a room in Schedule Poll.
2. Share the generated link with the people arranging the schedule.
3. Participants enter a nickname and select the times when they are available.
4. View the times with the most overlap based on the submitted availability.

You can use it for study groups, team projects, meetings, club activities, or any plan that needs several people to find a shared time.

Participants can respond for 24 hours after a room is created. After that, the results remain available, but new responses cannot be submitted.
