Hello from SYU CAMPUS.

We have launched the **Roommate Board** to help you find someone to live with in a Sahmyook University dormitory. Open [Find a Roommate](/campus/roommates) in the [Campus](/campus) menu. This notice brings together board instructions, privacy information, and advance notice of revised Terms.

## Find a roommate and write a listing

- Use **filters** for dormitory, room occupancy, residence period, and lifestyle preferences to find suitable listings.
- Listings require a dormitory, room occupancy, number of people sought, residence period, and recruitment deadline. Sleep and wake times, cleaning, calls, sleeping habits, smoking, heating and cooling, sharing belongings, and an introduction are optional.
- Contact the author through the **Kakao open-chat** link on a listing that interests you.
- Use **Manage My Listings** to edit a listing, mark recruitment complete, or delete it. When a listing is completed, deleted, expired, or hidden by an administrator, other users cannot see the listing or its contact link.
- You can **report** inappropriate listings for operator review.

## Verify your university email without separate registration

When you first use the board, verify your **@syuin.ac.kr university email** by opening the login link sent to it. There is no registration form or password to create. If you open the link in a different browser, you may need to enter the verified email address again.

Select **Keep me signed in for 30 days in this browser** to enter directly when returning in the same browser. Leave this unchecked on a shared device and log out afterward. A session without this option lasts for up to 12 hours.

Listings, lifestyle information, and contact links are shown only to verified users. Email verification confirms ownership of the address; it does not guarantee enrollment or eligibility for dorm admission. Follow the university's official dorm application and roommate assignment procedures.

## Interface and flow improvements

- Your previous screen and draft content are kept when you leave and return to a tab, reducing unnecessary repeated loading.
- You can leave through **‹ Campus** on the left. Logout is on the same line as the board menu.
- Filters are grouped into dormitory and residence period, and lifestyle preferences. The writing form is organized into basic information, residence and recruitment periods, optional lifestyle preferences and introduction, contact details, and consent to share personal information.
- We removed the unnecessary Cancel action from the university-email entry screen and added a home icon to the Campus menu.

## Privacy information and consent to sharing

Before creating or editing a listing, read about **recipients, purposes, data items, retention periods, and how to refuse or withdraw**, and explicitly consent to sharing personal information. You may leave every optional lifestyle field and introduction blank. Refusing sharing consent does not prevent authentication or viewing listings. The consent time and notice version are retained as evidence and are not shared with other students.

The [Privacy Policy](/privacy) explains the data and purposes for authentication, listings, and reports; retention and deletion; disclosure between users; processing arrangements and overseas processing; and deletion requests. **Logging out or deleting a listing does not delete your Firebase authentication user.** To request deletion of personal information, use Contact the site at the bottom of the page or [email the operator](mailto:singhic@outlook.kr).

The improved privacy notices and sharing consent apply to this feature. They do not treat users as having agreed to the upcoming Terms early. Statutory user rights and operator responsibilities apply regardless of whether the revised Terms have taken effect.
