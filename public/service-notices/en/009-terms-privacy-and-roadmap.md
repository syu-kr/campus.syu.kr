Hello from SYU CAMPUS.

As the service has added features, we have updated the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy to reflect how the service currently operates.

This revision clarifies the features already offered and how their data is processed; it does not request new permissions.

---

## Terms and Privacy Policy changes

The Terms of Service now cover campus tip suggestions, site inquiries, schedule polls, and push notifications.

The Privacy Policy explains the tools used to operate the service and the information they process in more detail, including Firebase, Firestore, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Analytics, and Kakao Maps.

The main changes are:

- More detailed information on processing inquiries and campus tip suggestions
- Added explanations of room information and participant availability entered in schedule polls
- Added information on push notification tokens and notification delivery records
- Explicit explanations of Firebase, Firestore, and Firebase Cloud Messaging use
- Security measures and limitations of liability aligned with actual operation

You can read the revised documents here:

- [Terms of Service](/terms)
- [Privacy Policy](/privacy)

## Timetable builder page added

We have added the timetable builder to the Academic Info menu.

This feature lets you plan your semester timetable and see your class times at a glance. There are still details to refine, and we will keep improving it to help with course planning.

---

## Graduation requirements in preparation

We are organizing the information needed to check graduation requirements more accurately.

There are many conditions to check based on admission year, major, and completion category. This is taking some time, but we will make a usable version available as soon as possible.

After the graduation requirements feature is complete, we plan to proceed with the app release.

## Looking ahead

We will continue improving SYU CAMPUS to make campus information quicker and easier to find.

If you encounter any inconvenience or incorrect information, please contact the site.
