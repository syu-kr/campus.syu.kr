Hello! We have added **public transit information** for the area around Sahmyook University.

You can now check bus arrivals directly in SYU CAMPUS!

---

## New features

### Gate-area stops

- **Main gate inbound / Main gate outbound**: Bus information toward Damteo Hill
- **Rear gate inbound / Rear gate outbound**: Bus information toward Taereung International Skating Rink

### Available information

- Bus route number
- Arrival time
- Next bus information
- Distance to the stop, measured in stops
- Seat occupancy (available/moderate/crowded)
- Whether it is a low-floor bus

### Convenient features

- **Refresh**: Check the latest information when you need it
- **Responsive design**: Optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop
- **At a glance**: Switch between four stop tabs

### Visual indicators

- **Red border**: Very close (one stop away), indicating that you should prepare to board
- **Blue border**: General bus information
- **Gray border**: No expected arrival information

---

## Where to find it

Open **Campus > Bus Info > Public Transit**.

---

## Details

- **Data source**: Gyeonggi Province Public Data Portal
- **Update interval**: 10 seconds
- **Supported buses**: All buses near the main and rear gates

---

## Please note

- "No expected arrival information" appears when no buses operate at that time.
- Bus information may change depending on traffic.
- Please also check official apps or websites for accurate arrival times.
