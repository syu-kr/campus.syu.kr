We are correcting descriptions that could misrepresent existing data processing and clarifying the load-testing and timetable-sharing Terms clauses.

## Privacy clarifications: November 3, 2026

The following descriptions in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) and [Notifications and privacy information](/more/privacy) are corrected.

- A note used for AI classification is the additional note entered by the submitter, not a separate internal memo written by the operator. Admin-triggered classification redacts selected submission fields and excludes the dedicated contact field and roommate reports.
- Schedule response edit tokens, schedule room deletion tokens, and timetable share deletion tokens are kept on the device and sent with the relevant request to verify permission. The corresponding feature documents store token hashes.
- We explain the roles of GA analytics cookies and the available opt-out methods and limitations in supported browsers. Clearing cookies or blocking third-party cookies alone is not described as stopping every analytics request.
- We remove the Hobby runtime-log viewing-window example, which did not establish this project's actual retention. Service log viewing and the provider's own retention are separate.

These corrections explain existing processing. They do not introduce additional collection or change the AI classification scope, analytics behavior, or retention periods.

## Further Terms amendment: December 4, 2026 at 00:00 KST

- Notice date: **November 3, 2026**
- Effective date: **December 4, 2026 at 00:00 Korean time**
- Scope: automated load testing in Article 5 and timetable sharing in Article 6 of the [Terms of Use](/terms)
- Reasons: to align the Korean and English scope of personal server testing and describe the information actually stored for timetable sharing.

The already announced **November 4, 2026 revision keeps its effective date**. This further amendment changes only the two clauses below. Their existing wording remains in place until the further amendment takes effect.

## Article 5 load testing: existing and upcoming wording

| Version | Wording |
| --- | --- |
| Existing wording in the November 4 revision | Malicious crawling, scraping, automation, or server load generation |
| Wording effective December 4 | Malicious crawling or scraping. Load testing the production service with automated tools or bots without the provider's prior permission. You may test in an isolated environment that you control. |

Testing in an isolated environment that you control is permitted. Automated load testing of the production service requires the provider's prior permission. This clause does not grant access to other systems or permit other prohibited conduct.

## Article 6 timetable sharing: existing and upcoming wording

| Version | Wording |
| --- | --- |
| Existing wording in the November 4 revision | Timetable sharing may store selected course IDs, year, semester, and request metadata for creating and loading share links. |
| Wording effective December 4 | Timetable sharing stores selected course IDs, academic year and semester, the courses assigned to each compared timetable, the active timetable and comparison mode, and an owner-token hash for verifying deletion rights. |

This describes the information stored in current timetable share documents. Handling ordinary server requests is distinct from retaining browser information in a timetable share document. Browser information collection is not added to match this description.
