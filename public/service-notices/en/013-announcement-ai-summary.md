Hello from SYU CAMPUS.

We have added **AI summaries** to help you check notices more quickly.

Some academic, scholarship, and campus notices now include a short AI-generated summary. Before opening a long notice, you can get an overview and decide more quickly whether it is relevant to you.

---

## What changed?

AI summaries appear below notice titles in notice lists and search results.

A summary may include:

- The main content of the notice
- The intended audience or students who should check it
- Actions such as applying, submitting, or checking information
- Deadlines or important dates
- An importance indicator

Notices with high importance use a more noticeable color, making potentially urgent notices a little easier to distinguish.

---

## Not every notice has a summary

AI summaries are generated in sequence from the original notices.

Some new or older notices may therefore not have a summary yet. Notices without summaries remain available through their title, publishing department, date, and original source link, as before.

---

## Always check the original notice too

AI summaries are supplementary information for quickly reviewing notices.

Always check the original notice for important details such as application periods, required documents, scholarship conditions, and academic procedures. The university's original notice is the authoritative source.

We will continue improving SYU CAMPUS to help you find notices more quickly and accurately.

Thank you.
