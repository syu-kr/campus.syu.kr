Hello from SYU CAMPUS.

More people have recently been using SYU CAMPUS for notices, cafeteria menus, shuttles, timetables, and other features. Thank you to everyone who uses the service and sends feedback.

---

## We will act on your feedback as quickly as possible

We regularly review bug reports and suggestions sent through Contact the site.

Although we cannot implement every suggestion immediately, we will prioritize problems that cause difficulties in actual use and improvements that help many people, and work to address them as quickly as possible.

If you encounter difficulties, incorrect information, or a feature you would like us to add, please use **Contact the site** at the bottom of the page.

## The Android app is currently in testing

The SYU CAMPUS Android app is being tested before its official release.

We will make it available as soon as possible after checking installation, startup, notifications, and the stability of its main features. Please bear with us while we prepare a stable release.

## Share it with other students

As more students discover and use SYU CAMPUS, we can identify needed improvements across a wider range of devices and environments more quickly.

If the service has helped you, please share it with other students. We will continue improving it based on your interest and feedback.

Thank you.
