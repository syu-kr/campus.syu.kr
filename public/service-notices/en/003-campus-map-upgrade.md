Hello from the SYU CAMPUS team.

To make the **Campus Map** more useful, we would appreciate your help collecting **building information and facility photographs**.

---

## What we need

### Room information and photographs for each floor of each building

- **Detailed floor plans**
- **Photos of individual rooms, including classrooms, laboratories, and offices**
- **Photos of corridors, stairs, and elevators on each floor**
- **Locations of key facilities, including drinking fountains and stairs**

### How to submit

**Please send all materials to the email address below.**

**Email**: singhic@outlook.kr / Subject: [SYU CAMPUS]Building name

**File naming format**

```
Building name_Floor_Description.jpg
Example) Daniel Hall_1F_Stairs.jpg
         John Hall_Floor plan.jpg
```

Find your classrooms before course registration.
