Hello! **SYU CAMPUS**, an integrated information platform for Sahmyook University students, has officially launched. 🎉

We have brought the information you need together in one place to make campus life more convenient and efficient.

---

## Features available now

### 📚 Notices

- **Academic notices**: Important academic information, including course registration, grades, and academic announcements
- **Campus notices**: Facility maintenance, events, and campus news
- **Scholarships**: On-campus and external scholarship announcements and application information
- **Service notices**: SYU CAMPUS updates and announcements

### 📖 Academic calendar

- Semester dates at a glance, including the start and end of classes and examination periods
- Weekly and monthly calendar views
- Quick access to today's schedule

### 🍽️ Cafeteria menus

- Weekly student cafeteria menus for breakfast, lunch, and dinner
- Data updated daily

### 📞 Contacts

- Telephone numbers for major university departments, including academic affairs, student support, and the library
- Quick search

### 🚌 Shuttle schedules

- Shuttle timetables for travel to and from campus
- Service information by route
