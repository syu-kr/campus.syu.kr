Hello from SYU CAMPUS.

The much-awaited **Graduation Self-Check renewal** is complete.

The previous graduation checker needed maintenance to improve accuracy and usability. In this update, we reorganized the data using the 2025 academic handbook and department-specific verification materials, and rebuilt the course selection and credit-checking flow.

We also fixed small bugs and inconvenient layouts. The service should now be much more comfortable to use.

---

## Graduation Self-Check renewal

The new graduation page lets you select your admission year, college, department, admission type, and major track to view reference requirements that match your situation.

The renewal includes:

- Step-by-step selection of graduation requirements based on admission year and department
- Checks for total earned credits, required liberal arts, required major courses, elective major courses, and other key credit categories
- A checklist for non-credit requirements, including examinations, chapel, practical training, and certifications
- Curriculum course selection for departments whose data has been verified
- Automatic entry of the credit totals for selected courses
- Official sources and the date they were last checked
- A button for reporting incorrect information directly

Course data currently comes from the verified 2025 curriculum. Students admitted in other years may also use course selection, but must confirm their actual curriculum with SU-WINGs and their department office.

---

## More comfortable on desktop

Graduation Self-Check has many choices and course lists, making it more comfortable to use on a large screen.

It is also available on mobile. When entering the page, you will see guidance recommending desktop use. You may continue on mobile or return to the previous page.

We have also improved the timetable builder for wide screens.

- See the weekly timetable and selected courses side by side
- Use a wide course selection area below the timetable
- View the course list in multiple columns on wide screens
- Keep the existing Add Course button and bottom sheet on mobile

These changes make it easier to compare selected courses with your timetable while planning classes.

---

## Other changes

This update also includes several improvements beyond the graduation checker renewal.

- Direct access to the graduation requirements page from the Academic menu
- Work to make eligible code open source
- Improvements to some page widths, layouts, and loading screens

We have also been tidying up less visible parts of the service. This will help us check and respond to future data changes or errors more quickly.

---

## Please check

Graduation Self-Check is a **reference service**.

Your actual graduation requirements may differ because of transfer admission, a change of department, double majors, minors, teacher training, department exceptions, or curriculum changes. Always confirm your final graduation status through:

1. Your official completion record in SU-WINGs
2. Guidance from your department office
3. A consultation with the Academic Support Team or responsible department

If you find incorrect information or a typo, use **Report incorrect information** on the graduation page. We will check it and make corrections as promptly as we can.

SYU CAMPUS is a service built together with our fellow students, affectionately called Duyu.

Please tell us whenever you find an inconvenience, incorrect information, or an idea for improving the service. Your feedback will help us make campus services more comfortable and reliable.

Thank you.
